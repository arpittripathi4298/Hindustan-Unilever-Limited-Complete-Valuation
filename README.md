# Hindustan-Unilever-Limited-Complete-Valuation

## 📌 Project Overview
This repository contains a comprehensive financial model and valuation analysis of Hindustan Unilever Ltd. (HUL), one of the leading Fast-Moving Consumer Goods (FMCG) companies in India. The project involves analyzing historical financial data and forecasting future performance up to the year 2031 to determine the company's intrinsic value and assess its financial health.

## 🎯 Objective
The primary goal of this project is to apply advanced corporate finance concepts and data analysis techniques to real-world corporate data. It aims to uncover the fundamental drivers of HUL's profitability, capital efficiency, and market valuation, providing strategic insights into its business model.

## 📊 Key Methodologies & Features
This financial model incorporates several critical analytical frameworks:

* **Discounted Cash Flow (DCF) Analysis:** Built a robust DCF model projecting Free Cash Flows. Calculated the Weighted Average Cost of Capital (WACC) to discount future cash flows and arrive at the absolute intrinsic valuation of the company.
* **DuPont Analysis:** Deconstructed Return on Equity (ROE) to analyze the underlying drivers of profitability. This includes evaluating operating margins, tax burdens, and asset turnover ratios to pinpoint operational efficiencies.
* **Relative Valuation (Comparable Company Analysis):** Benchmarked HUL against industry peers (e.g., ITC) using trading multiples such as EV/EBITDA, EV/Revenue, and Price-to-Earnings (P/E) to understand market sentiment and relative pricing.
* **Beta Calculation & Risk Assessment:** Calculated the stock's Beta by analyzing the historical price correlation between HUL and the NIFTY-50 index, quantifying systematic risk and market volatility exposure.
* **Financial Forecasting & Common Size Statements:** Developed integrated historical and forecasted income statements, balance sheets, and cash flow statements. Utilized common-size statements to track granular cost behaviors, including raw material and power/fuel expenses.

## 📂 Repository Contents
* `Hindustan Unilever Limited.xlsx`: The core Excel workbook containing all raw data, historical statements, forecasts, and valuation models.
